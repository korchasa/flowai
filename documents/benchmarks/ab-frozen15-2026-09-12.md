## What this delta is NOT

- **Not statistically significant.** Paired by instance, flowai and baseline
  differ on 10; flowai leads on 8. Exact binomial, two-sided, p ≈ 0.11 —
  closer than August's p ≈ 0.34, still short of the usual bar. 15 instances
  cannot carry a 15-vs-5 solve count as a general claim.
- **Baseline is a month old.** The bare arm was not re-run; drift in the hosted
  model between 2026-08-09 and 2026-09-12 is folded into the delta. The two
  flowai readings a month apart agreeing (13/45 and 15/45) is weak evidence that
  such drift is small, not proof.
- **Headroom-conditional** (FR-BENCH-SWE.POOL2 honesty rule): the pool was
  frozen to keep instances the bare arm does NOT solve reliably. A delta on it
  is a mechanism finder, never a general-effect claim.
- **Selected at a different operating point.** The freeze used the 20-min budget
  and the retired `sonnet` referee; this cell ran at 40 min under `gpt-5.6-sol`.
- **Stochastic gate.** Every flowai turn after `$plan` is emulator-authored, so
  the arm carries turn-level variance the baseline does not.
