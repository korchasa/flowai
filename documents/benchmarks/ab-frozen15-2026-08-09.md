## What this delta is NOT

- **Not statistically significant.** Paired by instance, the arms differ on 10;
  flowai leads on 7. Exact binomial, two-sided, p ≈ 0.34. The 13-vs-5 solve
  count looks larger than 15 instances can carry.
- **Headroom-conditional** (FR-BENCH-SWE.POOL2 honesty rule): the pool was
  frozen to keep instances the bare arm does NOT solve reliably. A delta on it
  is a mechanism finder, never a general-effect claim.
- **Selected at a different operating point.** The freeze used the 20-min budget
  and the retired `sonnet` referee; this pair ran at 40 min under `gpt-5.6-sol`.
- **Stochastic gate.** Every flowai turn after `$plan` is emulator-authored, so
  the arm carries turn-level variance the baseline does not.
