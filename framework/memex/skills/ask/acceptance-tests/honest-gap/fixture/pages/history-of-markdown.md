# The History of Markdown [ANC:mx-source:history-of-markdown]

Source summary covering the origin and adoption of Markdown.

## Key Points

- [REF:mx-concept:markdown | Markdown] was created in 2004.
- [REF:mx-person:john-gruber | John Gruber] is the primary author of [REF:mx-concept:markdown | Markdown].
- [REF:mx-person:aaron-swartz | Aaron Swartz] independently designed atx-style headers (`# Heading`) which Gruber adopted.
- The CommonMark specification was published in 2014 to formalise syntax.
